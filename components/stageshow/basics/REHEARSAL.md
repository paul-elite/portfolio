# The "basics" demos: rehearsal notes

Four scenes for the four basics articles (`/projects/typography-basics`, `colour-basics`, `ai-basics`, `product-basics`), ids `basics-type`, `basics-colour`, `basics-ai`, `basics-product`.

**Started from:** the kit's `close-up` (one control, one consequence) for type, colour and product; `ai-chat` for AI (same thread, composer and streaming).

**Stories**
- **Type (about 12s loop):** line height 1.0 → 1.5, the paragraph opens; tracking 0 → −3%, the headline pulls together; both put back.
- **Colour (about 12s loop):** white text on four steps of one blue; the ratio is computed from the colours (2.0, then 5.4 "Passes AA", then 10.4 "Passes AAA"); back to the pale step.
- **AI (plays once):** a loose ask and its all-purpose answer already in the thread; a specific ask is typed and sent, Thinking, a specific answer streams in.
- **Product (about 13s loop):** six features, 18 weeks; three switched off, the total rolls to 8; switched back on.

**Checked (2026-11-28):** filmed with `scripts/film.mjs --gate` in the Stageshow preview at the design width, and a four-frame sheet of each at 390.

**Gate lines left, and why**
- `uneven inset` on `ss-window__bar`: the kit Window's own title bar, in every scene.
- `loose stack` on "Line height" over its control: a field label above a segmented control, not a title over its subtitle; the gap is 6px.
- `dark slab` on the colour sample at its deepest step: the deep blue is the subject of that scene.
- `covers: the cursor sits on "1.5" as it presses`: on purpose, the hand presses the middle of what it clicks (Paul's rule for these scenes).
- `jump` on the `Count` digits in the product scene: the kit's digit reel.
- `steps in its box` on the first question in the AI scene as the thread moves up.

**Not checked:** Safari; the dark theme; the scenes inside the live article at phone width (only in the preview).
